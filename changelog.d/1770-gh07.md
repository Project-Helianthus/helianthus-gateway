## Prove the composed scrape with one retained cut, one store and one namespace

The route test now scrapes the real route with a canonical cut published through the hub, a persisted metric store and a namespace tree of synthetic drivers, with no injected domain and no prebuilt native observation. Its expectation is declared in a test-only file, and the list no longer ships in the gateway binary. The namespace publishes non-SI points (bar, degrees Celsius, litres per minute, kilowatt-hours, revolutions per minute, percent) and the test checks their SI series and their original values afterwards. The scrape covers the 1025th value and age, both native pairs, a stale point and the per-field availability family. Test only; the gateway's output is unchanged.
