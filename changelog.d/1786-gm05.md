## Use the SemReg revision with the namespace roster and the full element catalog

The gateway now requires the SemReg revision that lists namespaces, exposes the installation root and continues a captured cut by identifier, and the three SemReg mapping-gate scripts name that revision. The Matter binding feed ledger lists the dispositions of the complete element catalog of that revision (1070 items, up from 190), and the published feed schema bounds the ledger's `requested` and `dispositions` arrays at 4096 instead of 256; `resources` and `operations` keep their bound of 256. No tool is added or renamed (#1786).
