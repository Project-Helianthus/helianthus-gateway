## A stale or unavailable owning source stays visible on the semantic series

On a fact key with candidates from several sources and no open conflict, the scrape now shows the state and age of the owning source's candidate, including when it is stale or unavailable, and exports no value for it. A lower-priority source's fresh reading no longer replaces it. The existing selection still applies when the owner has no candidate on the key or no owner is known.
