## Prometheus exports served-cut values, durable slots and driver points

The scrape keys `helianthus_semantic_evidence_age`, `helianthus_semantic_fact_state`, `helianthus_semantic_fact_value` and `helianthus_semantic_open_conflicts` by resource, fact definition and qualifier values. Those four names stay the same. The `domain` and `fact_id` labels leave them; `fact`, `resource` and `resource_kind` are added, and a percent on a ratio fact is a ratio from 0 to 1. Capacity stays in ampere-hour. Every other semantic family keeps its labels and series.

Durable slots use that same resource, fact and qualifier identity. A retired slot stays reserved. A document stored under the old key is not read. Given a store location, the metrics registry creates a missing store and reuses it across restarts. A scrape only reads the store.

Every numeric point a driver publishes is exported with its age. There is no count limit of our own, so a 1025th value series of one target is exported like the others. The protocol label is the family string the driver published. A point with no documented instance index carries no index label. The metric inventory and the coverage page list the same families.
