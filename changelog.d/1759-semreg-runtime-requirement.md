## Move the SemReg requirement onto the runtime packages

The gateway now requires the helianthus-semreg revision that contains the runtime packages, including the tree, identity and life packages. The semantic snapshot read no longer derives the retired pack availability view. Element availability is read from a lens result when the capture supplies one: an asset the cut lists as ungraphed contributes no capability, and a lens fact's evaluation is the availability of that candidate. A capture without a lens result still judges a capability from the capability instance on the snapshot for the shipped packs, and a fact from the snapshot evaluation. The served snapshot document is unchanged when the lens result carries the same capability instance and the same evaluation.

Energy quantities that the packs now declare in joule are published and exported in joule: a watt-hour counter is multiplied by 3600, and a kilowatt-hour counter by 3600000. Inverter cabinet temperature and storage pack temperature are published in kelvin, degree Celsius plus 273.15. Storage state of charge is published as a ratio, the percent reading divided by 100. The infrastructure energy gauges export that joule quantity unchanged; the previous kilowatt-hour multiplier is gone. Series names are unchanged.
